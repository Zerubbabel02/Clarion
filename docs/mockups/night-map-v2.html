Night Map

The map is still your home screen, in Night Watch’s dark colours. This version gives the map more room, puts the Flare button on its own island, and adds a clean Map tab.

What changed

  • The greeting is one small glass island: your letter button, then “Good evening, Zerubbabel” and “All quiet in your circle”. It is only as wide as the words.
  • “Fatima · Live · See” sits beside Find my circle. When two or more people share, it shows “+1” and opens Share.
  • The Flare button, the “Reaches…” chip and the radius bar are one island, above the tabs.
  • While you hold a Flare button you see a countdown (“sending in 0.9 s”) and orange ripples. Let go early and nothing is sent.
  • The tabs are Home, Share, Map, You. Home, Map and You are full pages. Share is a pop-up.
  • The Map tab shows only you and the people sharing with you. You can drag and zoom it, and tap a name to see the way to them. It has a small Flare button with the same countdown.
  • The You page is grouped like your real Profile screen: your photo and @username on top, then Account, Flare, Alerts and Appearance. Each row opens its own page.
  • Upload a profile photo, and set a username friends can search to add you. Adding someone now works by username, phone number or email, the same as your app.
  • Flare ringtone: Clarion SOS tones, your phone’s ringtones, or your own sound file. Tap Play to hear each one.
  • In Share, “Sharing with you” comes first. Tap a person and the Map tab shows the way from you to them.

Try this

  1. On Home, press and hold “Hold to send a Flare”. Watch the countdown and ripples, and let go early once to see that nothing is sent.
  2. Tap “Reaches everyone within…” to switch between distance and trusted circle. Drag the bar to change the distance. The orange dots are the neighbours a Flare would reach.
  3. Tap See on the Fatima pill. The Map tab opens with the way from you to her.
  4. On the Map tab, drag the map, zoom with + and − (or pinch, or your mouse wheel), and tap the target button to come back to yourself.
  5. Hold the small orange Flare button on the Map tab.
  6. Press “Friend shares location” beside the phone. The pill changes to “Chidi +1” and opens Share.
  7. Open You and tap the camera to upload a photo. It also appears in your greeting on Home.
  8. Tap Username and try “fatima.sani” (taken) or a new one. Then open Who gets my Flare, tap Add someone and search “ibro” or “0803”.
  9. Open Flare ringtone, press Play on each SOS tone, then add a sound file from your device.
  10. Press “Neighbour sends a Flare” beside the phone to see an incoming alert.

Neighbours, places and the map are sample data. Nothing here touches your real app.
